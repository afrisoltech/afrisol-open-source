# Open Source Roadmap

## Foundation
- Community documentation, governance, sponsorship and responsible disclosure.
- Basic linting, tests, dependency review and least-privilege CI after a verified implementation PR.

## Developer resources
- Small independently developed examples for low-bandwidth, accessible and mobile-first software.
- Documentation for APIs, localization, interoperability and responsible AI.

## Community collaboration
- Label beginner-friendly issues and publish maintainer-reviewed contribution opportunities.
- Seek feedback from African developers, students, startups and institutions.

Items are plans, not claims of released features or committed delivery dates.
