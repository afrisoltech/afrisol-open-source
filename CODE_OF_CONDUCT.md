# Code of Conduct

Afrisol Technologies welcomes contributors from across Africa and the world. Be respectful, inclusive, constructive, and considerate of differing backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and disclosure of another person's private information are prohibited.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove harmful content, and restrict participation where necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel at https://www.afrisoltech.com; avoid posting personal information in public issues.
