# Project Governance

Afrisol Technologies maintains this repository and its public contribution process. Changes are proposed through issues and pull requests; maintainers review correctness, security, licensing, documentation, accessibility, and relevance to African markets. Sponsors may suggest priorities but cannot purchase acceptance, privileged access, or influence over security decisions.

Security fixes may be coordinated privately before publication. Maintainers record substantial decisions in issues or pull requests. Commercial APOS systems and customer environments are outside this repository's governance scope.
