# Afrisol Technologies — Brand Identity Guidelines

## Approved messaging

**Primary corporate slogan:** Empowering Africa Through Technology.

**Supporting campaign message:** African Innovation. Global Impact.

**Legacy slogan:** Passionate Solutions (retained as brand heritage; do not use as the default slogan on new materials).

## Logo integrity

The original Afrisol Technologies folded-ribbon **A** icon is the authoritative mark. Preserve its complete geometry, orange crossbar, blue structural forms, highlights, proportions, orientation and spacing. Do not abbreviate, redraw, simplify, crop, recolour, rotate or replace the icon. Derivative logo treatments must be based on verified original master assets, not AI-generated approximations.

## Brand palette

- Afrisol orange: `#F15A24`
- Afrisol blue: `#0F6BC0`
- Supporting navy, black, white and neutral greys may be used for contrast and accessibility; define exact supporting values in an approved design-token release.

## Typography

Preferred corporate typeface: Lato, with appropriate licensed distribution and accessible system fallbacks. Preserve legibility and clear visual hierarchy. The supplied reference also names Lotto Love 4; do not distribute or embed it without verified licensing and approved usage.

## Logo variants and deliverables

When original editable master artwork is available, export **without altering the icon**:

| Asset | Preferred deliverables |
| --- | --- |
| Primary horizontal lockup | SVG and transparent PNG at 1x, 2x and 4x |
| Stacked lockup | SVG and transparent PNG at 1x, 2x and 4x |
| Icon only | SVG, 1024, 512, 256, 128 and 64 px PNG |
| Web favicons | 16, 32, 48 px PNG and ICO |
| App / social avatars | 1024, 512, 256 and 180 px PNG |
| Open Graph / social card | 1200 × 630 PNG |
| Print | Print-ready PDF and vector SVG; CMYK conversion only after proofing |

Ensure transparent assets have true alpha transparency, not a black background; use light and dark background variants with contrast checks. Maintain safe space around the full icon and wordmark. Avoid tiny slogans on favicon and app-icon assets; omission of the slogan at small sizes is not shortening the icon.

## Usage

Use the primary slogan in the company website, GitHub profile, sponsor materials, business communications and formal identity. Use the supporting message selectively for campaigns and international partnerships. The slogan does not replace the registered corporate name.

## Intellectual property and publication

Logo masters are corporate brand assets and are **not automatically licensed under this repository's MIT software licence**. Confirm artwork ownership, fonts and redistribution permissions before committing master logo files. Do not publish private APOS materials.

## Acceptance

A brand export is approved only after visual comparison against the original icon, vector/path inspection, correct colour sampling, responsive-size checks and background/contrast tests. This document is guidance, not a claim that production logo packages have been exported.
